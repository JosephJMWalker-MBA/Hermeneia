# Project Axioms

**Status:** Historical / interpretive orientation  
**Authority:** Non-constitutional; where an axiom conflicts with active constitutional law, ADRs, or specifications, current authority wins. See `docs/01_Authority_Index.md`.

1. Observations are immutable.
2. Interpretations evolve.
3. Perspectives accumulate.
4. Narratives transform readers.
5. Humans remain essential contributors.
6. Provenance is mandatory.
7. Understanding is preferred over certainty.
8. Dialogue expands the field.
