# Corpora

The `corpora/` directory preserves validated or example corpus artifacts used by Hermeneia.

A corpus is an enduring epistemic substrate preserving source material, observations, provenance, context, and relationships according to the active architecture.

Hermeneia began with *The Great Gatsby*, and Gatsby remains a useful real-corpus validation fixture. It is not a special architectural dependency and does not define the product boundary.

```text
Gatsby
= founding corpus + useful fixture
!= Hermeneia's only intended corpus
```

Hermeneia is explicitly multi-corpus. Literature, manuscripts, theology, civic documents, legal records, standards, archives, and other governed source collections may participate when licensing, provenance, and corpus boundaries are handled correctly.

Derived essays, reports, guides, and other projections may be regenerated from governed study records.

Canonical evidence and governed lineage endure.

Only validated bundles should be treated as validated corpus artifacts. Placeholders and examples must say so explicitly.
